About

This portal is for the students of UET Mardan. It shows all the sports, technical and cultural events in one place so students can easily see what is happening and join the events they are interested in.

Events

Code Sprint Hackathon

A 24-hour coding contest where teams build an app.

Cricket Tournament

Departments play against each other on the university ground.

Career Seminar

Guest speakers give advice about jobs and internships.

Cultural Day

Music, food stalls and performances from our local culture.
